# C6 — fastest routes to publication (checked 23 Nov 2026)

Priority: speed. Figures below come from publisher pages or aggregators found on 23 Nov 2026; confirm on the journal site before submitting (APCs change).

| Rank | Venue | Why fast | Indexing | Cost (approx.) | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (do now, in parallel) | **bioRxiv** (Microbiology) preprint | Online in ~2–4 days; citable DOI; free | Google Scholar, Europe PMC | Free | Establishes priority immediately; allowed by all options below |
| 1 | **F1000Research** | Published after pre-publication checks, typically 2–3 weeks; peer review is open and happens *after* publication | PubMed/Scopus once it passes review (2 approvals) | ~US$1,000–1,300 by article type; LMIC discounts/waivers | Good: meta-research with open data/code is its core content |
| 2 | **Access Microbiology** (Microbiology Society) | Open, transparent review; preprint-first model | DOAJ, PMC | ~£700–950, charged only on acceptance; waiver requests considered | Good: microbiology, methods/reporting audits accepted |
| 3 | **Microorganisms** (MDPI) | Median ~15 days to first decision, ~34 days to publication (2020 publisher figure) | Scopus, SCIE, PubMed | Check current APC (MDPI APCs are typically CHF 2,000–3,000) | Good topical fit; conventional peer review |
| 4 | **PeerJ** (Life & Environment) | Median ~30 days to first decision, ~74 days to publication | PubMed, SCIE | Check current APC | Good; sound-science review (null/reversed results fine) |

**Recommendation:** post to bioRxiv the day the authors approve the text, and submit the same day to **F1000Research** if fastest visible publication matters most, or to **Microorganisms** if a conventionally peer-reviewed, indexed paper within ~1–2 months matters most.

Before submission (all venues): convert `manuscript_v2.md` to .docx (Word); fill the Declarations placeholders (competing interests, funding, author contributions); add the repository DOI (e.g. archive the repository on Zenodo for a DOI); upload Figure 1 (`results/figure1.pdf`), Tables 1–2 and supplementary files from `manuscript/supplementary/`.

Sources: f1000research.com/for-authors/article-processing-charges; authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com (What is F1000Research?); microbiologyresearch.org/publishing-costs; doaj.org/toc/2516-8290; peerj.com/benefits/peer-review-timeline; MDPI *Microorganisms* reviewer acknowledgement 2020 (PMC7911680).
